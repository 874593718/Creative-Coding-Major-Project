WATER LILY POND

Open index.html in the extracted project folder. All runtime resources are local.
The automatic cycle changes season every 600 frames (about 10 seconds at 60 fps).
The buttons allow a quick preview. Auto pauses or resumes the season timer.

MAIN PARTS
sketch.js: initialisation, per-frame updates, drawing order and window resizing.
time-mechanic.js: the season timer, target lily sizes and colours, falling objects,
and simple drawing functions for petals and maple leaves.
perlin-lilies.js: the original pond, random layout and Perlin-noise movement.
Only drawLily() was adapted to read seasonal sizes and colours.
audio-mechanic.js and input-controls.js remain empty integration hooks.

COURSE MATERIAL CHECK
Week 2: RGB colours, HTML IDs and document.getElementById().
Weeks 3-4: functions, objects, arrays, conditions and loops.
Weeks 6 and 8: p5 drawing, random/noise, transforms and window resizing.
Week 11: lerp() easing and frame-based animation.
The added petal and leaf drawings use ellipses, triangles and lines, rather than
Bezier curves or custom vertex shapes. No new classes or extra libraries were added.
These supplied tutorials support the techniques listed here; they are not a formal
assessment whitelist. The original teammate's noise and layout code was preserved.

The seasonal growth and winter retreat are simplified artistic representations.
This version was made with AI assistance and should be reviewed and understood.
The copied README.md and docs describe the original Week 1 project, not these additions.
